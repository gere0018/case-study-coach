About this prototype

  • Outside Claude, this runs in basic mode: it checks your answers and gaps with simple rules. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
  • Inside Claude, coaching, previews and the hiring-manager check are written by Claude, so remove confidential details first.
  • Reading a Figma link is simulated and only works inside Claude.
  • Your progress is saved in this browser only. Use Save and exit to leave and come back.
  • The coach never adds metrics, outcomes, decisions or quotes you didn’t write.
